Define / Problem statement04

Problem statement

Everything learned so far, in one statement.

Problem statementBLUORNG's marketing and content team lacks a structured way to pre-plan ads and organic content, and has no reliable feedback on which content works. As a result, shoot days produce fewer usable ads than they could, Meta ad spend follows habit instead of evidence, and Instagram posting relies on instinct rather than on what has worked for BLUORNG and comparable premium streetwear brands.

How might we...

HMW 1

Show the team which of its ads and posts work, and why?

HMW 2

Learn from what works for comparable premium streetwear brands?

HMW 3

Plan ad angles before the shoot, so one shoot day gives the most ads?

HMW 4

Suggest what to post on Instagram, based on BLUORNG's own history?

HMW 5

Help the team spend Meta budget with confidence?

HMW 6

Keep every suggestion premium and on-brand?

TakeawaySix questions to ideate on. None of them assumes a solution yet.
Define / Defined design goals05

Design goals

What any solution must achieve, and how we'll measure it.

Overall goalGive BLUORNG a system that suggests what to make and how to make it, for Instagram and Meta ads.
G1
Show what works

Reveal which BLUORNG posts and ads perform, by type, with reasons.

Measure: Name top ad type in under 1 min
G2
Learn from the market

Bring in what works for premium streetwear competitors.

Measure: Competitor patterns weekly
G3
Suggest what to make

Recommend ad ideas and organic posts for the next drop.

Measure: Ideas ready before planning
G4
Plan the shoot

Turn ideas into a shoot plan that serves posts and ads.

Measure: Ad cuts per shoot from ~4 to 10+
G5
Spend with confidence

Back proven angles with ad budget.

Measure: Lower cost per sale
G6
Protect the brand

Keep every suggestion premium and explain it.

Measure: Founders approve most ideas
G7
Close the loop

Record what each drop teaches for the next one.

Measure: Every drop adds learning
TakeawayWith goals set, ideation can begin. Every idea will be checked against these seven goals.

Ideate / Ideation / SCAMPER09

SCAMPER

Questioning the current process one letter at a time.

Ideate / Ideation / Idea pool10

Eight ideas worth testing

Clustering the session outputs into eight distinct directions.

C1
Content analyst hire

A full-time analyst studies results and competitors and advises the team.

C2
Creative agency

Outsource ad strategy, competitor research and ad creative.

C3
Tracking template

A shared Notion or Sheets tracker and a weekly review meeting.

C4
Testing playbook

A written process for testing ad angles and planning shoots.

C5
AI insights dashboard

Software that analyses BLUORNG's page and ads plus the Meta Ad Library, then suggests what to make.

C6
WhatsApp digest bot

A weekly message summarising top posts and ads.

C7
Social listening tool

An off-the-shelf subscription for trends and competitor tracking.

C8
Creator network

A roster of creators for regular UGC ads.

TakeawayEight different directions: people, process, partners and products. Now they need filtering.

Ideate / Final direction14

The direction: BLUPRINT

Chosen because it meets the most design goals at a cost that fits BLUORNG.

Final ideaAn AI-assisted insights dashboard, powered by Claude AI, that constantly analyses BLUORNG's Instagram page, its Meta ad results and the Meta Ad Library, then tells the team what type of ads and posts to make, how to make them, and how to plan the shoot to get the most out of it.

BLUPRINT in use

How each persona's day changes with the chosen direction. Figures are illustrative.

Naina, Marketing LeadDrop planning, Monday

Opens BLUPRINT before the planning meeting. It shows creator try-on ads cost 28% less per sale than studio lookbooks over the last three drops, and suggests four ad angles for the new drop. She takes it straight into the meeting.

AV
Aditya, Performance MarketerMid-campaign

Gets an alert: the lookbook ad's frequency is 4.1 and clicks are falling. BLUPRINT shows a competitor's close-up ad pattern doing well in the Ad Library and suggests two replacement hooks from footage already shot.

RS
Riya, Social Media ManagerWeekly planning

Instead of a blank page, she gets five post ideas based on BLUORNG's best-saved posts: styling carousels and fabric close-ups, with reasons and the best times to post.

KM
Karan, PhotographerShoot day

Opens the shot list on his phone. It covers the lookbook, four ad angles in 9:16 and 4:5, and close-ups for ASMR posts. He finishes with 14 usable ad cuts instead of four.

TakeawayEvery scenario moves a decision from instinct to evidence, before money is spent.
