Overview / Project proposal04

Project overview

A UX case study, from understanding the problem to choosing a solution direction.

Industry partner
BLUORNG, New Delhi

Premium Indian streetwear label, founded 2020. Drop-led, Instagram-first, runs Meta ads for every drop.

My role
UX designer, embedded

I work alongside BLUORNG's content and marketing team, so I could observe the content process first-hand.

Duration
4 months

Research, synthesis, ideation and design, ending in a tested prototype.

Background

BLUORNG builds hype for every drop through Instagram content and Meta ads. Each drop needs fresh posts, a shoot, ad creatives and ad budget. The team decides what to make mostly on experience and instinct, and results are spread across different tools.

Project aim

Understand how BLUORNG's marketing and content team decides what to create for organic social media and Meta ads, find where time and money are lost, and design a solution that helps the team make better content decisions.

TakeawayThe project starts with a question, not a solution: how does BLUORNG decide what content to make?

People adopt tools that are useful and easy.

So: Any new way of working must save the team time.

2010
Kaplan & Haenlein

Brands must take part in social media, not broadcast.

So: Participation beats announcements.

2012
Berger & Milkman

High-arousal emotions drive sharing.

So: Emotion matters as much as format.

2012
de Vries et al.

Vivid and interactive posts get different responses.

So: Format and interactivity change results.

2012
Kim & Ko

Entertainment and interaction build luxury brand equity.

So: Premium brands need more than conversion content.

2015
Ashley & Tuten

Brands use functional, emotional and experiential strategies.

So: Ads can be grouped by creative angle.

2016
Godey et al.

Social builds awareness and image, then loyalty.

So: Measure recall, not only sales.

2016
Lemon & Verhoef

Experience spans the whole customer journey.

So: Content serves discover, desire, buy and share.

2019
Amershi et al.

Guidelines for human-AI interaction: explain, allow correction.

So: Any automated help must explain itself.

2023–25
Meta, creative is targeting

Diverse creative improves cost and reach.

So: Ad variety is a planning problem.

Discover / Problem identification17

How I spotted the problem

The problem came from first-hand observation, then had to be tested with research.

My vantage point
Working inside the team

As part of BLUORNG's content process, I sat in planning meetings, shoots and edit reviews, and saw how ad and post decisions get made.

What I kept seeing
  • Planning starts from moodboards and instinct, not results.
  • Ads are cut from whatever the lookbook shoot produced.
  • The marketing team can see an ad is failing, but not why.
  • Competitor ads are checked by hand, then forgotten.
  • Money spent on making and running ads is hard to justify.
Initial problemBLUORNG's marketing team spends heavily on making and running Meta ads, but has no reliable way to know which ideas work. A system is needed that tells the team what kind of content to make for Instagram and Meta ads.
TakeawayAn observation is not yet a finding. The next steps break the problem down and test it.

Discover / Problem analysis19

Why does it happen? Five whys

Asking why, five times, from a real situation in the last drop.

Why 1Why did last drop's ads underperform? They all looked alike.
Why 2They were all cut from the same lookbook footage.
Why 3The shoot was planned only for the lookbook.
Why 4Nobody knew which ad angles worked, so none were planned.
RootThe team has no evidence of what works to plan shoots and ads around.
TakeawayFixing ads means fixing planning, and fixing planning needs evidence.

Research plan

Two groups, two methods: a survey for the audience, interviews for the team.

Objectives
  • Learn how the team decides what to make, and what data it uses.
  • Find where time and money are lost in a drop cycle.
  • Learn what content BLUORNG's audience watches, saves, trusts and buys from.
  • Understand what would help the team plan better.
Methods
  • Online survey of BLUORNG's audience, 80 responses.
  • 10 semi-structured interviews with BLUORNG's team, 20 to 45 minutes.
  • Observation during a drop cycle, from inside the team.

Define / Problem statement45

Problem statement

Everything learned so far, in one statement.

Problem statementBLUORNG's marketing and content team lacks a structured way to pre-plan ads and organic content, and has no reliable feedback on which content works. As a result, shoot days produce fewer usable ads than they could, Meta ad spend follows habit instead of evidence, and Instagram posting relies on instinct rather than on what has worked for BLUORNG and comparable premium streetwear brands.

How might we...

HMW 1

Show the team which of its ads and posts work, and why?

HMW 2

Learn from what works for comparable premium streetwear brands?

HMW 3

Plan ad angles before the shoot, so one shoot day gives the most ads?

HMW 4

Suggest what to post on Instagram, based on BLUORNG's own history?

HMW 5

Help the team spend Meta budget with confidence?

HMW 6

Keep every suggestion premium and on-brand?

TakeawaySix questions to ideate on. None of them assumes a solution yet.
