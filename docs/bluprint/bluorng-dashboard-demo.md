## Step 8: 10 content ideas
1. **Price and drop-time FAQ story** (High) — Hook: “Price, sizes, time. Everything before Friday.” — Static — FAQ → clear facts → reminder CTA
   Why: Price and availability asked in 51 of 1394 comments; CTA posts average +5% vs no-CTA posts.
   Caption opener: Everything you need before the drop.
2. **Size guide from the comments** (High) — Hook: “What size should I get? Answered.” — Carousel — Real question → answer → proof (fit on 3 bodies)
   Why: Size and fit questions in 44 of 1394 comments.
   Caption opener: Your most asked question, answered once.
3. **Restock alert done right** (High) — Hook: “It's back. For 48 hours.” — Reel — Proof of demand → return → deadline
   Why: Restock requests in 42 of 1394 comments; 2% of comments show frustration (sell-outs, bots, price).
   Caption opener: You kept asking. It's back, briefly.
4. **Drop countdown series** (High) — Hook: “72 hours. One piece. No restock.” — Reel — Countdown → tease → date and time
   Why: Drops & launches average 14,527 engagements per post (+30% vs account average, 19 posts); urgency hooks average +11%; 'next drop dates' asked in 98 comments.
   Caption opener: Three days left before it's gone.
5. **Styling one piece three ways** (High) — Hook: “One hoodie. Three cities.” — Reel — One product → three looks → save prompt
   Why: 'More styling / fits' asked in 36 of 1394 comments; Reels average +25% vs account.
   Caption opener: Save this for your next fit.
6. **Collab reveal in three frames** (Medium) — Hook: “Two names. One drop. Guess who.” — Carousel — Teaser → reveal → details
   Why: Collaborations average 13,968 engagements per post (+25% vs account average, 7 posts).
   Caption opener: You asked. They answered.
7. **Prime-time anchor post** (Medium) — Hook: “The one post to see this week.” — Reel — Best format (Reel) at the best slot
   Why: One planned hero post a week in the best slot: Fri is the best day by average engagement; Reels are the best format (+25%).
   Caption opener: This week's main moment.
8. **Behind the print** (Medium) — Hook: “From sketch to screen print in 30 seconds.” — Reel — Process timelapse → maker → final piece
   Why: Behind-the-scenes or process asked in 38 of 1394 comments; Product & craft average 12,862 engagements per post (+15% vs account average, 29 posts).
   Caption opener: Every piece starts on paper.
9. **ASMR fabric close-ups** (Medium) — Hook: “Listen to 400 GSM.” — Reel — Sensory hook → slow detail → product reveal
   Why: Product & craft average 12,862 engagements per post (+15% vs account average, 29 posts); Reels average +25% vs account.
   Caption opener: No music. Just the fabric.
10. **Store-day story experience** (Medium) — Hook: “This was the queue at 9 am.” — Reel — Scene → crowd → product moment → invite
   Why: Store & events average 12,999 engagements per post (+16% vs account average, 32 posts); 'Stores in more cities' asked in 33 comments.
   Caption opener: Mumbai showed up. Who's next?
