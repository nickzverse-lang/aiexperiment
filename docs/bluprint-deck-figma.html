Background and problem

Today, results sit in Insights, Ads Manager and Shopify, and decisions happen on WhatsApp.

Organic content
Drop teasersLookbooksCampaign filmsBehind the scenesStore launchesCollabsCulture posts
Paid content, Meta ads
DropsRetargetingStore footfallEvergreen products
Initial briefDrop-led streetwear teams have no structured, evidence-backed way to decide what to make for Instagram and Meta ads. Effort and spend go on guesswork, wins aren't repeated, and organic and paid run as separate worlds.
Aim

Turn performance data, brand identity and trend signals into on-brand content suggestions and ready-to-shoot briefs, for organic and paid.

Scope and outcome

In scope
  • Instagram organic ideas: Reels, carousels, stories and series like ASMR or story experiences
  • Meta ads ideas: angles, hooks, variations and fatigue alerts
  • Insights on what worked, briefs and a drop calendar
  • Desktop web app plus a mobile companion for set and approvals
Out of scope
  • Building the machine-learning model or backend
  • Auto-posting or buying ads inside the tool
  • YouTube and X, kept as future scope
  • Redesigning the e-commerce site
Hi-fi prototype

BLUPRINT web app and mobile companion, researched and usability-tested.

Design system and IA

Documented structure, navigation, labels and UI components.

Usable recommendations

Validated changes BLUORNG can apply even without building the product.

Which formats win?

Fashion data favours Reels. Large accounts favour carousels.

Instagram organic

Reach is algorithmic, driven by watch time, shares and saves. The hook in the first 3 to 4 seconds matters, and series build habit and recall.

Meta ads

Advantage+ automates targeting, so the creative itself now does the targeting. The team's main lever is creative diversity.

Creative fatigue

Results drop as people see the same ad again. The team needs fresh variations, and to know when to refresh.

Five whys

Why did the last drop's ads underperform?

Why 1Why did the last drop's ads underperform? The creatives looked very similar.
Why 2All of them were cut from the same lookbook shoot.
Why 3Only one shoot was planned, and it focused on the lookbook.
Why 4Nobody planned ad angles like UGC, detail or social proof before the shoot.
RootNo step turns "what wins" into a pre-shoot plan for both organic and paid. This is the design opportunity.
