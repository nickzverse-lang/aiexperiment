Insight 1
Data without direction

The team checks numbers weekly but rarely turns them into what to make next.

75% check, 29% act
Insight 2
The idea treadmill

Drops demand constant novelty, and idea block is the top-ranked problem.

41 pts, rank 1
Insight 3
Two teams, one feed

Organic winners rarely reach ads, and ads are cut from one shoot.

46% rarely or never
Insight 4
Spend follows habit

Most ad money goes to lookbook and scarcity angles that cost more per sale.

77% of spend
Insight 5
Brief is a voice note

Informal briefs lead to late changes, re-edits and missing formats.

13 h lost in one week
Insight 6
Save-worthy beats scroll-worthy

Followers save styling and ASMR detail, and stop for creators.

49% / 41% / 57%
Insight 7
Show me why

AI help is welcome only when it explains itself and stays on-brand.

54%, 6 of 6
